Can your family use Utah Fits All?

✓ Where you live
Your child lives in Utah.
✓ Grade
Any child from kindergarten to grade 12. bina teaches from Pre-K to Grade 10.
✓ Income
No income limit. Places are limited, so applying early with complete paperwork helps.
✓ Dates
Utah has one application window a year. For 2026–27, returning students could apply from March 1 and new students from April 1, and applications closed May 1. Awards were announced June 16 and scholarships funded July 15. Utah hasn't published the 2027–28 dates yet.

Utah Fits All in plain words

The Utah Fits All Scholarship is a Utah state program. When your child is awarded a scholarship, the state puts money into an Odyssey account for your child's education. You spend it on approved expenses, including tuition at a private online school like bina. Funding renews each school year.

FAQ

Is bina an approved Utah Fits All provider? +

Yes. bina is listed in the Utah Fits All marketplace on Odyssey, and Utah families have paid us through it since 2025.

Does the scholarship cover all of bina's tuition? +

It covers most of it. With the full $8,000, families on the yearly plan top up about $231 a month. Your exact award depends on your family, and our financial aid team will confirm your numbers.

When can I apply? +

Once a year. In 2026–27, returning students could apply from March 1, new students from April 1, and the window closed on May 1. Utah hasn't published the 2027–28 dates yet, and we'll share them as soon as it does.

Is it worth talking to bina before the window opens? +

Yes. Families who apply early with complete paperwork have the best chance. We help you get everything ready ahead of time.

Can my child start before the scholarship is funded? +

Yes. You can enroll and pay bina directly now, then switch to scholarship funding once it's in place.

Will someone help me with the application? +

Yes. Our financial aid team guides you through every step, from the application to your first payment.

Do you offer a sibling discount? +

Yes. Each additional child gets $1,500 off tuition.

Is bina responsible for this program? +

The Utah Fits All Scholarship is run by the state of Utah, not by bina. Program amounts, eligibility and deadlines are subject to change. Always confirm with the official program site before you rely on a number here.
