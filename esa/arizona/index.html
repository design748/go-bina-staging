Can your family use the Arizona ESA?

✓ Where you live
Your child lives in Arizona.
✓ Grade
Any child from kindergarten to grade 12 can apply. bina teaches from Pre-K to Grade 10.
✓ Income
No income limit.
✓ School
Your child doesn't need to have attended public school before. While using ESA funds, your child studies with bina instead of a public or charter school.
✓ Dates
Arizona accepts applications all year. Processing can take up to 45 days. Funding runs in quarters (ending Sep 30, Dec 31, Mar 31 and Jun 30), and the date you sign your ESA agreement sets the quarter your funding starts.

The Arizona ESA in plain words

The Empowerment Scholarship Account is run by the Arizona Department of Education. When your child is approved, the state puts money into an account for your child's education every quarter. You spend it through ClassWallet on approved expenses, and tuition at an online school like bina is one of them. Unused funds roll over while your child stays in the program.

FAQ

Is bina an approved Arizona ESA school? +

Yes. bina has been an approved school on ClassWallet since November 2024, and the Arizona Department of Education confirmed our registration in June 2026.

Does the ESA cover all of bina's tuition? +

It covers most of it. On the yearly plan, families usually top up about $231–$314 a month, depending on their award.

Is there an application deadline? +

Arizona accepts applications all year. The date you sign your ESA agreement sets which quarter your funding starts, so applying earlier means funding starts sooner.

How long does approval take? +

Up to 45 days after you apply, according to the Arizona Department of Education.

How does bina get paid? +

We send you an invoice. You create an order in ClassWallet, the state reviews it and pays bina directly, usually within about a week.

Can my child start before the ESA is approved? +

Yes. You can enroll and pay bina directly while your application is being reviewed, then switch to ESA funding once it's approved.

Will someone help me with the application? +

Yes. Our financial aid team guides you through every step, from the application to your first payment.

Do you offer a sibling discount? +

Yes. Each additional child gets $1,500 off tuition.

My child is in public school now. Can we still apply? +

Yes. Your child can apply while in public school and studies with bina once your ESA is active.

Is bina responsible for this program? +

The Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) is run by the state of Arizona, not by bina. Program amounts, eligibility and deadlines are subject to change. Always confirm with the official program site before you rely on a number here.
