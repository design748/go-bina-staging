FAQ

What is an ESA? +

An Education Savings Account is money your state puts aside for your child's education. You can spend it on approved expenses, including tuition at an approved school like bina.

Which states can I use at bina? +

Arizona, New Hampshire, Idaho, Utah, West Virginia and Wyoming. Choose your state above for the details.

Does the award cover all of bina's tuition? +

It covers a large part of it, and you top up the rest each month. Awards depend on your state and your family, and our financial aid team will confirm your numbers.

Who applies, me or bina? +

You apply to your state's program on its official website. We go through the application with you and send everything the state needs from bina.

Will someone help me through the process? +

Yes. Our financial aid team guides you from your first question to your first payment.

Can my child start before my ESA is approved? +

Yes. You can enroll and pay bina directly while your application is being reviewed, then switch to ESA funding once it's approved.

Do you offer a sibling discount? +

Yes. Each additional child gets $1,500 off tuition.

My state isn't listed yet. Can my child still join bina? +

Yes. Families in any state can join bina with direct tuition. Ask us about your state, and we'll let you know what's possible.

Is bina responsible for these programs? +

Each ESA program is run by its own state, not by bina. Amounts, eligibility and deadlines are set by the state and can change. Always confirm with your state's official program site before you rely on a number here.
