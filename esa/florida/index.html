Florida's FES-UA scholarship and bina: a parent's guide

If your child qualifies for Florida's Family Empowerment Scholarship for Students with Unique Abilities (FES-UA), you can ask for bina tutoring costs to be paid back from your scholarship account.

  • ✓ For Florida students with a qualifying unique ability
  • ✓ bina tutoring can be reimbursed as part-time tutoring
  • ✓ Our financial aid team helps you apply and prepares the invoice the state needs

FES-UA in plain words

FES-UA is a Florida education savings account for students with unique abilities, managed by Step Up For Students (SUFS). The state deposits money into an account each quarter, and families spend it on approved costs, including part-time tutoring.

  • Award: depends on your child's grade, county and needs. Our financial aid team will go through your numbers with you.
  • Rollover: unused funds roll over each year, up to a $50,000 account cap.
  • How bina fits: bina isn't paid directly from the account. You pay bina, then ask SUFS to reimburse you.

Can your child use FES-UA?

✓ Where you live
Your child lives in Florida.
✓ Age
At least 3 by December 31, 2026, and under 22 on September 1, 2026.
✓ Unique ability
An IEP from a Florida school district, or a qualifying diagnosis from a licensed physician, physician assistant, APRN or psychologist (for example autism, ADHD, Down syndrome, or a speech or language impairment).
✓ Income
No income limit.
✓ Dates
New 2026–27 applications close November 15, 2026.

How bina tutoring is covered

  • ✓ bina's live online classes are invoiced as part-time tutoring, which can be taught online.
  • ✓ Part-time tutoring is capped at 720 hours a year for grades K–3 and 900 hours a year for grades 4–12.
  • ✓ You can request reimbursement for tutoring prepaid up to four months ahead.
  • ✓ SUFS reviews every request under state rules. Whether a request is approved, how much is repaid and when is decided by the state, not by bina.

What bina does

  • ✓ Talks you through the scholarship and your numbers before you apply
  • ✓ Sends an invoice with everything SUFS needs

  1. 1 Step 1: Talk to bina With bina Book an ESA call with our financial aid team to go through how FES-UA works with bina.
  2. 2 Step 2: Create an EMA account On Florida's site Create an EMA account on the Step Up For Students portal. One account covers the whole family.
  3. 3 Step 3: Apply for FES-UA On Florida's site Apply for FES-UA and upload your documents: two proofs of Florida residency, your child's birth certificate or passport, Social Security numbers for you and your child, and your child's IEP or diagnosis paperwork. New applications close November 15, 2026.
  4. 4 Step 4: Accept your scholarship in EMA On Florida's site Accept your scholarship in EMA by December 15, 2026, and set up how you want to be reimbursed (direct deposit, PayPal or check).
  5. 5 Step 5: Pay bina and request reimbursement Together Pay by card or bank transfer, then submit bina's invoice and proof of payment under the Reimbursements tab in EMA. Allow up to 60 days for processing.

What goes on your bina invoice

  • ✓ Your child's name
  • ✓ The subject and the dates covered
  • ✓ The teacher's name and license number, and the company name (bina)
  • ✓ The total for the period as one amount, the transaction date and proof it's paid in full

Tips: keep every receipt, reply within 30 days if a request goes on hold, and check the SUFS Unique Abilities Purchasing Guide or ask for pre-authorization if you're unsure.

FAQ

Does FES-UA pay bina directly? +

No. You pay bina, then ask Step Up For Students to reimburse you from your scholarship account.

Will FES-UA cover all of bina's tuition? +

FES-UA covers bina as part-time tutoring, up to the yearly hour limits. Your award depends on your child's grade, county and needs, and our financial aid team will go through what that means for your family before you apply.

Who qualifies? +

Florida students with a qualifying unique ability, shown by an IEP or a diagnosis from a licensed professional. There's no income limit.

When is the deadline? +

New 2026–27 applications close November 15, 2026, and new students must accept the scholarship by December 15, 2026. Returning families renew between February 1 and April 30 each year.

How long does reimbursement take? +

Up to 60 days after all documents are in. 2026–27 funds can be spent until June 30, 2027, and requests must be submitted by July 31, 2027.

Will someone help me with the application? +

Yes. Our financial aid team guides you through every step, from the application to your reimbursement request.

Is bina responsible for this program? +

FES-UA is run by the state of Florida and managed by Step Up For Students, not by bina. Program amounts, eligibility, deadlines and reimbursement decisions are subject to change. Always confirm with the official program site before you rely on a number here.
