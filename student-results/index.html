How STAR measures learning

Every number on this page comes from Renaissance STAR, an independent assessment taken by millions of US students. Here's what it measures and what each term means.

Independent

Built and scored by Renaissance Learning

Renaissance sets the questions and the national comparisons, so every result is measured independently.

Adaptive

It finds each child's real level

Questions get harder or easier as a child answers, so the result reflects what they can do.

Nationally normed

Compared with students across the US

"On track" means on track compared with students in the same grade nationwide.

FAQ

What is STAR, and why does it matter? +

STAR is an adaptive reading and math assessment by Renaissance Learning, used with millions of US students. Because an outside company builds and scores it, every number on this page is measured independently.

What is a "scaled score"? +

A single number for what a student can do, on one continuous scale across all grades. It only goes up when ability goes up, which is why growth in scaled-score points is the clearest way to show a year of learning.

What is the "national benchmark"? +

Renaissance counts a student as at or above benchmark when they score in the top 60% of US students for their grade (the 40th percentile or higher). It's the standard line schools use for "on track".

How do you report these numbers? +

Growth figures cover the 108 students assessed in both October 2025 and May 2026 at the same level. Spring results cover everyone assessed in May. We only report groups of 10 or more students, and every figure is a school-wide average, so no individual student's results appear on this page.

Where do these numbers come from? +

Every figure comes from Renaissance STAR assessments taken during the 2025–26 school year (October 2025 to May 2026). Individual results vary, and past results don't guarantee future ones. You can learn more about STAR at renaissance.com.
