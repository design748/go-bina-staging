What bina families think about bina

Every year, we ask bina families how their child is doing, how we're doing and what they'd like to see next. Here are all the results, including the score we're working to raise this year.

FAQ

Who did you ask, and when? +

We invited every bina family. 103 families answered the whole-school survey (a 44% response rate), and 108 parents answered a separate middle-school survey. We report the two surveys separately, and every figure says which one it comes from.

What is a Net Promoter Score of 80? +

Net Promoter Score is the standard measure of how likely people are to recommend something. Parents who answer 9 or 10 count as promoters, 7 or 8 as passives, and 0 to 6 as detractors. The score is the share of promoters minus the share of detractors, so it runs from −100 to +100.

Is 80 a good score for a school? +

Yes. Independent School Management (ISM) studied 28,477 parent responses from 95 private schools and rates parent scores of 70 and above as excellent.

What should I keep in mind when reading these scores? +

Families who answer surveys tend to be the most engaged ones, so read the scores with that in mind. Every figure is a school-wide average, and no individual family or child is shown. For results measured independently, see our student results.

Where do these numbers come from? +

All figures come from bina's own family surveys: a whole-school survey answered by 103 families and a middle-school parent survey with 108 responses. They're reported separately and shown as school-wide averages. The parent score ranges come from Independent School Management (Ideas & Perspectives, Vol. 47, No. 5, May 2022), which you can check at isminc.com.
